Experiential Immanence

Look.

The road, the wind, a thought, a memory. All of it appears.

Names appear too. They are not under what is seen, or above it. They are beside it.

When a thought says “this is how it is”, let it appear. Do not hold on to it.

These words are also only words.

The system, in words
